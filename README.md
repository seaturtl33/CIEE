## Cyber Dragon
A hybrid bullet hell/roguelite game where you fight waves of yokai in a future Japan.
## How to play
Move the player using the arrow keys. Press Z to shoot. Shoot at enemies to destroy them while dodging their attacks as well. After each wave, you player chooses one of three options for an upgrade for their character.
## Controls
* `Arrow Keys`: Move character
* `Z Key`: Shoot projectile
* `Enter`: Confirm
* `Esc`: Exit
* `Space Bar`: Pause and unpause
